# ASEAN Trade Repository Candidate

This is a source-derived canonical markdown candidate for review and chunking
experiments. It is not approved runtime retrieval material.

## Source-Derived Notes

The ASEAN Trade Repository is described as a single ASEAN-level access point for
trade-related information from ASEAN Member States.

The source explains that the ATR links to National Trade Repositories, where
member-state governments provide and maintain national-level trade information.

The source organizes information around ATIGA Article 13 topics, including
tariff nomenclature, tariffs, rules of origin, non-tariff measures, trade and
customs laws, procedures and documentary requirements, rulings, trade
facilitation practices, and authorized economic operators.
